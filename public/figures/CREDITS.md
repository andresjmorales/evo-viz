# Figures

Silhouettes are from [PhyloPic](https://www.phylopic.org/), mostly by T. Michael Keesey, and are in the public domain (CC0 1.0 or Public Domain Mark 1.0). `longi.svg` is an uncredited CC0 PhyloPic image.

`erectus.svg` is the PhyloPic *Homo ergaster* figure and `heidelbergensis.svg` is *Homo rhodesiensis*.
