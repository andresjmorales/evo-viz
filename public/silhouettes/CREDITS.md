# Silhouettes

Walking figures of *Homo erectus pekinensis*, *Homo neanderthalensis*, and *Homo sapiens* are by T. Michael Keesey, from [PhyloPic](https://www.phylopic.org/), dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The *Australopithecus* walking figure is an original teaching drawing for evo-viz (also CC0).
